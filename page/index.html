Signing boundary

A launch request is not authorization. Nexus hashes the factory, calldata, value, predicted addresses, economics, and warnings into one plan ID. A wallet opens only after that exact plan is revalidated and simulated. Token launches are permanent and can lose all value; Nexus and its external protocols are unaudited.
